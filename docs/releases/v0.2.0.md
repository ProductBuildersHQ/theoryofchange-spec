# Release Notes v0.2.0

Released: 2026-11-01

Ties the chain from activities to impact to the numbers people steer by and are
told. A program or product can name a North Star and the guardrails that watch it,
each audience can lead with its own short list of numbers, and 18 new lint rules
check that the chain is filled in. The logic model can now be drawn as columns
with a real arrow wherever one thing leads to another.

This release has one breaking change: the `intermediate` outcome level is now
`medium_term`. See [Upgrading](#upgrading).

## Highlights

- **Headline metrics.** A North Star, an optional one metric that matters, and
  guardrails for each program or product. A short, ordered, worded headline list
  for each audience, drawn from the same indicators, so the board, funders, and
  members can lead with different numbers and still see the same figure.
- **A checked chain.** 66 lint rules, 18 of them new, that look for an activity
  that leads nowhere, a link and intervention that disagree, an audience told
  about outcomes but given no numbers, and a North Star that is an output or sits
  above the ceiling of accountability.
- **A layered logic model diagram.** Columns from inputs to impact, a box for
  each item, and arrows that follow the theory. Written as D2 and as Mermaid.
- **Composed indicators.** A number such as cost per applied change is worked out
  from the latest value of its parts, and an estimate is shown as an estimate.
- **One series of levels.** Short, medium, and long term, with `medium_term`
  replacing `intermediate`.

## New Features

### Headline metrics

- **North Star.** A pathway's `focus` names the indicator it steers by
  (`northStarId`), an optional `oneMetricId`, the `guardrailIds` that watch what
  chasing it could harm, and a `rationale`. A pathway can now be a `program` or a
  `product`.
- **Headline lists.** An audience's `headline` is an ordered list of indicators,
  each with an optional `label` and `note` worded for that reader.
- **Mission and vision.** `context.mission` and `context.vision` state purpose
  without making it part of the causal chain.
- **Tiers.** What an indicator measures decides its tier (activity, output,
  outcome, or impact). `Document.IndicatorTier`, `IndicatorServes`, and
  `IndicatorPathways` answer it once for every rule and report.
- **Commands.** `pathway set` takes `--north-star`, `--one-metric`, and
  `--guardrails`, and an audience's headline list is set with `--set headline=...`.
  Removing an indicator with `rm --cascade` also removes it from every headline
  list and focus that names it.

### Lint

18 new rules, 66 in all:

- **The chain** (`TOC-INT-003` to `TOC-INT-007`): every intervention says what it
  produces, leads to an impact, agrees with the links that name it, is measured,
  and states its resources.
- **Headline lists** (`TOC-AUD-002`, `TOC-HLN-001` to `TOC-HLN-004`): an audience
  told about outcomes is also given numbers, and a headline list is short, is
  reported to its audience, can be produced, and is chosen when there are many
  indicators.
- **North Stars** (`TOC-NSM-001` to `TOC-NSM-007`): a North Star measures an
  outcome at or below the ceiling of accountability, has a baseline, a target, and
  a guardrail, belongs to its own pathway, and is not also a guardrail.
- **Framing** (`TOC-CTX-001`): the theory states its mission or vision.

Every new rule is a warning or advice, so a theory that was GO stays GO.

### Reports and decks

- **`headline`** shows what each audience is told, with the latest figure, status,
  claim, notes, and caveats, and a matrix of who is told what. `--audience`
  narrows it.
- **`metric-tree`** shows, for each pathway, its North Star and guardrails and then
  its indicators from activity to impact, as a table and a diagram. `--pathway`
  narrows it.
- **`logic-model-layered`** and **`logic-model-d2`** draw the logic model as
  columns. See below.
- **Estimates.** An estimated value reads "estimate, on 2026-09-30" in every
  report, with its observation note.
- **Story report and deck.** They tell an audience its whole headline list, in its
  order and wording. They no longer take the first five indicators in file order.
- **Workshop deck.** Each logic model table is followed by a slide that draws the
  same logic model as columns with arrows.

### The layered logic model diagram

An input leads to the activities that use it, an activity to the outputs it lists
and then to the outcomes it targets, and an outcome to the outcomes its causal
links point to, so one box can lead to several and several can lead to one.
Within a column, boxes are ordered to keep arrows from crossing. A link that runs
back to an earlier stage is feedback. It is listed under the diagram and not drawn
across the boxes in between.

`logic-model-layered` writes Mermaid. `logic-model-d2` writes D2, and its grid
keeps every stage in its own column:

```bash
theoryofchange report logic-model-d2 theory.yaml --pathway pw-learning > lm.d2
d2 lm.d2 lm.svg
```

The existing `logic-model` table and `--diagram` are unchanged. The diagram is
written through [d2vision](https://github.com/grokify/d2vision)'s diagram spec.

### Composed indicators

An indicator can be worked out from others with `composedOf`: a `ratio`,
`difference`, `product`, or `sum`, such as a cost divided by the changes it paid
for. When the indicator has no observation of its own, its value comes from the
latest value of each part, and it is marked as an estimate if any part is. A
missing part or a zero divisor gives no value, so a partial figure is never
passed off as complete. Validation checks the operation, the number of parts, that
the parts exist, and that the arithmetic is not circular. It is arithmetic and not
a causal link. `indicator set` takes `--composed-op` and `--composed-of`.

The membership network example now has a mission and vision, a North Star and
guardrail for three of its program areas, and headline lists for the board,
funders, and members.

## Installation

```bash
go install github.com/ProductBuildersHQ/theoryofchange-spec/cmd/theoryofchange@v0.2.0
```

Go 1.26.5 or later. The optional programs are the same as in v0.1.0: `marp` for
decks, `mmdc` for diagrams, and `pandoc` for Word and PowerPoint. To draw the D2
diagram you also need the `d2` tool, and the command that writes D2 does not.

## Upgrading

### `intermediate` is now `medium_term`

The level is renamed so that short, medium, and long term read as one series.
There is no alias. A theory that still says `level: intermediate` fails
validation, once for each such outcome:

```
error VAL-SCH-001 /outcomes/6/level: value must be one of 'impact', 'long_term', 'medium_term', 'short_term', 'output'
```

Replace `level: intermediate` with `level: medium_term` in YAML, or
`"level": "intermediate"` with `"level": "medium_term"` in JSON. Other places that
change with it:

- Reports and diagrams say "Medium-term outcomes" where they said "Intermediate
  outcomes", and a Mermaid subgraph is `lv_medium_term`.
- In Go, `toc.LevelIntermediate` is `toc.LevelMediumTerm`.
- The embedded `theory-of-change` JSON Schema lists `medium_term`.

### More lint findings

A theory that was clean can now show new warnings and advice, such as
`TOC-INT-003` for an intervention that does not say what it produces, and
`TOC-NSM-007` for a program with no North Star. None is an error, so the result
is still GO. Use `lint --disable` to skip a rule you do not want yet.

## Compatibility

Files still carry `specVersion: "0.1"`. This version reads any `0.x`, so a
document written for v0.1.0 is read, and only its `intermediate` levels need to
change. Every new field is optional. Exit codes are unchanged.

## Known Limitations

- **Inputs are text.** The diagram's inputs come from an intervention's free-text
  `resources`, so one funding source cannot yet feed several activities by ID.
- **Wide diagrams.** With seven stages and long labels the layered diagram is wide,
  and text on a slide is small. Labels are not wrapped.
- **Feedback is listed, not drawn.** A link that runs back to an earlier stage
  appears in a list under the diagram.
- **Mermaid places its own columns.** Only the D2 form keeps each stage in a
  column.
- **Panels.** A slide with panels for background, goal, issues, assumptions, and
  critical success factors around the diagram is not built.
- The limitations of v0.1.0 for Google sharing, the USM and UFM contract, rendering
  tools, and judging still apply.
